-   Figure out ways to lose, and do the opposite.
-   Your environment sets your standards. Change the environment, change the behavior.
-   You want to feel more motivated? Change who you compare yourself to. 
-   **Compare yourself to the person you genuinely want to become.**
-   In the best things in life you never win. You win at business by staying in business. You win at marriage by staying in marriage.
