-   Deep work is very important in many professions
-   There are jobs where deep work is not important, for example if you’re CEO of a large enterprise 
-   In today’s world, we are full of distractions and we don’t value deep work enough
-   Deep work is getting more and more rare and therefore more and more valuable
-   **People fight desires/distractions all day.**
-   Willpower is limited and will drain the more distractions you fight.
-   **We need routines and rituals to have to use less willpower to not fall for distractions.**
-   **Great deep workers are famous for their routines.**
-   Think weeks, work retreats, and similar in different environments can be a great help.
-   Schedule relaxing time.
-   **The ability to focus deeply is a skill that must be trained**.
-   **Embrace boredom**.
-   **To learn to focus well, you need to, one, learn to concentrate better and, two, learn to overcome your desire for distraction.**
-   Practice “productive meditation”.
    -   Doing the excercise or taking a walk focus yourself on one thought. When you get distracted, get back to the thi fight. 
    -   Do this three times a week to improve your ability to focus.
-   Memorizing exercises train your brain and your ability to focus.
-   Reduce social media usage.
-   Track your work time and analyze how much deep work you’re doing.
