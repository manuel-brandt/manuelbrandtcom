### Key Takeaways:

-   **Always focus on the 1 thing that brings you the best results for your business, even if it’s not the most exciting.**
-   **When it’s easy, do more.** If something is working you need to go all in on this, because if you don’t, someone else who’s bigger than you will do what you do.
-   **Only do side hustles that can become a main hustle.**
-   **Act with urgency.** ~End of week~ -> End of day. ~End of month~ -> End of week.
-   Try to work every day but less long. Alex only had 11 days in 2024 where he worked less than 4 hours. “I got significantly more purpose and more drive than of a day watching movies or whatever.”
-   “I see wealth as a measure of personal growth. I see Entrepreneurship as the highest level of personal development.”
-   It’s not about the 5 persons closest to you but the 5 person you compare yourself with (which can influencers e.t.c as well).

### Other:

-   Think in shorter time frames.
-   Focus on less things but do them faster.
-   Supply and demand is key.
-   Take less vacations (Alex works 7 days a week, 10-16 hours a day).
-   For Alex it’s more effective to work every day but less instead of 5 days a lot and then 2 days do nothing.
-   Eliminate unnecessary things.
-   You need a spouse who supports you and doesn’t make you get away from work (“I want to cuddle, maybe finish your work”).
-   _Alex just works out 2 times a week now to focus more on work._
-   A business is like a sports team, it’s not a family. We are there to win. Give people the opportunity to do better, but if they are not, give them the opportunity to work somewhere where they will fit in better.
-   Problems are usually created long before. If your leads drop don’t look at what did you change last week, look at what did you change 6 months ago.
-   Try greyscale again.
