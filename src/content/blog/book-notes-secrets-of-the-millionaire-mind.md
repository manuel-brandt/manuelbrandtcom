### Money Blueprint

-   If as a child we got taught a bad picture of money or rich people, then it’s hard for us to get rich ourselves 

### Wealth Files:

-   Never blame, justify, or complain. 
-   Your goal needs to be rich, not to be comfortable. Only then you can get rich. 
-   Think big. (At Blogtec launching in different countries will help us grow because it’s thinking very big)
-   Focus on opportunities, not obstacles.
    -   “I have a motto. Action always beats inaction. Rich people get started. They trust that once they get in the game, the can make intelligent decisions in the present moment, make corrections, and adjust their sails along the way.”
-   Rich people admire and support rich people and are not jealous.
    -   “You don’t need to be perfect to be rich, you just need to realize when your thinking isn’t empowering you to be rich.”
-   Surround yourself by rich people.
    -   Note, can I find places/groups where I can surround myself with rich people?
-   Leaders earn more than followers. 
-   You must believe in your product’s value. If the value is high, then charge accordingly.
-   Rich people are not afraid of problems and challenges.
    -   “If you have a big problem in your life all that it means is that you’re a small person.”

### Last Notes:

Overall, I the book rather refreshed/deepened things that I knew already, rather than teaching me new things.

My main take aways are:

-   **Stop complaining that much.** I generally want to be a person that doesn’t complain. If it helps with being more wealthy, that’s an additional plus.
-   **Befriend with more rich people.** None of my friends are rich. I don’t meant that I can only have rich people, but I realized in the past as well that spending your time with rich and successful people motivates you and does something with your mindset. I think having close friends that are rich and successful could be very helpful for my own mindset.
